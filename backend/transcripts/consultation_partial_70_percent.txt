Doctor: Tell me what has been bothering you.
Patient: I have been getting headaches.
Doctor: When did you first notice them?
Patient: Maybe four or five days ago.
Doctor: How long does each headache usually last?
Patient: Usually around an hour, sometimes a bit longer.
Doctor: Where do you feel the headache?
Patient: Mostly around my forehead and behind my eyes.
Doctor: How bad is it when it is at its worst?
Patient: Maybe six out of ten.
Doctor: Anything else happening with the headaches?
Patient: Sometimes I feel a little sensitive to light.
Doctor: Are you taking anything regularly?
Patient: Only vitamin D tablets. I took paracetamol twice this week for the headache.
Doctor: All right. Have the headaches been affecting your sleep?
Patient: Not really. I can still sleep normally.
Doctor: Anything else you think I should know?
Patient: No, I think that's everything.
